About

Fairway Buddy gives junior golfers simple swing feedback and at-home drills, wherever they practice.

For swing feedback, Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, looks at frames from your video and uses Google's MediaPipe body tracking on your device to measure your swing. Treat tips as friendly guidance, not a replacement for a real coach. Version 1.0.

Acknowledgements

Swing feedback, drills and coach notes are generated by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.

Body-point tracking uses the MediaPipe Pose Landmarker by Google (Apache License 2.0), through the @mediapipe/tasks-vision package. It runs on your device. Claude asks it for measurements while it reviews your swing.

The logo and icons were drawn for this app.

Dedicated to every young golfer practicing in a backyard, a living room or a driveway.
